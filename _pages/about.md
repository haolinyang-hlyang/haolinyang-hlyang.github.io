I am currently a 2nd-year PhD student at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/), advised by [Weidi Xie](https://weidixie.github.io/). Prior to this, I obtained a B.S. in Computer Science from [Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications](https://www.bupt.edu.cn/), ranked 1/440. During my undergraduate studies, I was a research intern under the supervision of [Li Yi](https://ericyi.github.io/) at Tsinghua University.

My research interests lie in Computer Vision and Multimodal Learning. If you'd like to collaborate or have any questions, feel free to reach out to me via email or WeChat.
